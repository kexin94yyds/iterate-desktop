# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or decisions.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining evidence-driven.
- Protect private data and security reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disclosure of private data, and repeated disruption are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Use
GitHub's abuse-reporting tools for urgent platform abuse and contact the
repository owner privately for conduct concerns that should not be public.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
